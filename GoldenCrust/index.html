Company Background

Golden Crust Bakery is a small home-based bakery established in 2026 that specializes in freshly baked breads, pastries, cakes, and other homemade treats. The business started from a simple passion for baking and gradually grew by serving family, friends, and customers in the local community. Golden Crust focuses on providing fresh, affordable, and quality baked products while maintaining good customer service. As the business continues to grow, the company aims to improve its order and ingredient management to make its daily operations more organized and efficient.
